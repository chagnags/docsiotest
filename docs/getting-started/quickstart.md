Get your first documentation site live in under 30 seconds. No credit card, no setup, no code required.

## Sign up

1. Go to [docsio.co](https://docsio.co) and click **Get Started Free**.
2. Create an account with your email or GitHub.
3. You're ready to build.

No credit card required. The free plan includes one documentation site with 20 AI agent edits per month, custom domains, brand extraction, live preview, and hosted publishing.

## Paste your URL

1. Enter your website URL (e.g., `https://acme.com`).
2. Click **Set up my docs**.
3. Docsio maps your entire site and extracts your brand identity automatically.

The AI agent reads your website, detects your primary and secondary colors, downloads your logo and favicon, and identifies your typography. All of this happens in the background while you wait.

:::note
If your website is just a marketing site with no product information, you can upload internal documents instead. See [Uploading documents](/docs/guides/uploading-documents) for details.
:::

## Watch it build

The AI agent builds your entire documentation site in front of you. You'll see live progress as it:

- Extracts your brand colors, logo, and fonts
- Maps and scrapes your website content
- Writes documentation pages using real product information
- Builds your homepage with features and navigation
- Runs a full audit to check every link and page

The entire process takes 2-5 minutes depending on your website size.

```bash
# Example progress you'll see
Extracted brand: #1a1a2e, logo, 2 fonts
Mapped 32 pages on acme.com
Batch scraped 18 product pages
Created 14 documentation pages
Built homepage with real content
Writing footer with navigation links...
```

## Review and publish

1. Open the **Live Preview** tab to see your complete documentation site.
2. Navigate between pages, test dark mode, and check mobile layouts.
3. If you want to make changes, click **Ask the agent** and describe what you'd like to change.
4. When you're happy with the result, click **Publish**.

Your site goes live at `yourproject.docs.docsio.co` with automatic SSL in seconds. You can connect a custom domain later from **Settings → Domains**.

:::tip
The live preview is a fully interactive documentation site. Click links, open the sidebar, and navigate exactly as your readers will experience it.
:::

## What happens next

Your documentation site is now live and ready to share. You can:

- **Share the URL** with your team, customers, or the public.
- **Connect a custom domain** like `docs.acme.com` from Settings.
- **Make edits** by chatting with the AI agent in the editor.
- **Republish** whenever you make changes. Your URL stays the same.

The free plan gives you 20 AI agent edits per month. If you need unlimited edits, upgrade to Pro for $60/month per site.
